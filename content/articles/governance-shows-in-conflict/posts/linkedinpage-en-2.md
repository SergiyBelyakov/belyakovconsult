"Who sets the rule?" and "Who fixes an error?" sound like organisational questions. In practice they are questions about specific names.

Without names, answers last until the first reshuffle. Then a new analyst counts "the way they understood it", and the number conflict is back in the meeting.

Four roles are usually enough:
• data owner: the head of the area, approves definitions and changes
• data steward: knows day to day how the data works and keeps the indicator description
• data custodian: the team that implements the rule in the system or register
• data consumer: analysts, reporting teams, partners

In a small organisation one person can hold two or three roles. That is fine. The problem is a critical indicator with neither an owner nor a steward.

Start with one indicator: the one behind your last number conflict. Write down two names. That is already data governance, even without a hundred-page policy.
