Your data is 98% complete. What exactly is in it?

Completeness counts filled fields. It does not check whether a value can be used. "N/A", "0", "-", "01.01.1900" and "TEST TEST" all pass as filled.

So this time the placeholders became shrimp, three cats got laser eyes, and a capybara in a suit asked the only sensible question: how many values are usable, not how many are filled.

The serious part fits into one check. Take one key field in your register or dataset and count the values that are filled but meaningless. That share sits inside your completeness score, and it usually surfaces only when a report goes wrong.
