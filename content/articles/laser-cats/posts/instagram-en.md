Your data is 98% complete. So what exactly is in it?

Completeness tells you a field is filled. It does not tell you the value can be used. "N/A", "0", "-", "01.01.1900", "asdf" and "TEST TEST" all count as filled. (In this video they are shrimp. It felt fair.)

A 10-minute check: take one key field and count the values that are filled but mean nothing. That share is hiding inside your 98%.

Laser cats are optional. The check is not.
