Is 01/02/2026 January 2nd or February 1st?

Depends on who typed it. One source writes the day first, another writes the month first, and the report reads all of them with one rule. A batch of February records lands in January, and someone starts explaining a spike that never happened.

The sources and numbers in the video are illustrative. The fix is boring and it works: agree on one date format at the point of entry (2026-02-01) and note which format each source sends.

Same date, different month.
