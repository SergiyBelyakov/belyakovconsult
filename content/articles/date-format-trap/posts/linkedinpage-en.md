A small pixel-art reminder about dates.

01/02/2026 is February 1st in day-first files and January 2nd in month-first files. When a report combines sources that write dates differently, it reads all of them with one rule, and some records land in the wrong month. Nothing errors out. The chart simply shows a spike that never happened. (The sources and numbers in the video are illustrative.)

A practical check: look at records where the day is 12 or less. Those are the ambiguous ones. If your sources use different formats, agree on one format at the point of entry, ISO 8601 (2026-02-01), and document which format each source sends.
