How do you find duplicates in a register? Not by name alone: namesakes are common. Not by document number alone either: documents change.
<!-- next -->
You need a key from several fields: normalised surname and first name, date of birth, document number without the series, a tax ID if there is one. For near matches there is fuzzy matching.
