Happy Halloween. Is your register haunted?

Ghost records are entries with no real person behind them: duplicates, test users, people who left years ago, IDs that match nothing. Nobody sees them, yet every report counts them.

So tonight the ghosts are fluffy maltipoos, husky ninjas resolve them one by one (merged, archived, matched), the stakeholders are hedonist cats who only consume clean data, and capybara artists paint the result.

The serious part is a 10-minute ghost hunt: look for one person under two IDs, or test accounts still marked as active. Each one you find is a number in your reports that should not be there.

Count people, not ghosts.
