Happy Halloween. Is your register haunted?

Ghost records are entries with no real person behind them: duplicates, test users, people who left years ago, IDs that match nothing. Nobody sees them. Every report still counts them.

A 10-minute ghost hunt: look for one person under two IDs, or test accounts still marked as active. Each one you find is a number in your reports that shouldn't be there.

Husky ninjas are optional. Counting each person once is not.

Count people, not ghosts.
