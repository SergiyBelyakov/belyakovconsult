This video has a duplicate. Did you spot it before the capybara?

A 22-second video with four scenes turned out to have three unique ones. Nobody planned it, nobody noticed, and the scene count still looked fine.

Registers work the same way. The record count looks fine until someone checks how many of those records are actually unique people.

A quick check: count records and count unique IDs in the same table. If the two numbers differ, you have your own duplicate scene.

Comment "meta" if you noticed it first.
