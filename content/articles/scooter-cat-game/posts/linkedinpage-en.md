What if reading a chart felt like riding it?

Scooter Cat: Ride the Chart is a free browser game where each level is a chart type you meet in management reports: a trend with noise, a histogram where the median and the mean disagree, seasonality, Pareto, a truncated axis, a waterfall, a control chart and a forecast.

The point is not the score. After a few rides people start noticing what each chart is really saying: that January is not a failure, that the biggest step in a waterfall is usually cost, that a truncated axis can make a small change look dramatic.
