A meme for Monday, with a real data lesson inside.

"How many clients do we have?" The CRM says 6, the ERP says 7, and both are called the source of truth. In most cases the gap is a single record entered twice: a different phone format, a typo in the name, a missing middle initial.

A five-minute check: pick the client you suspect is duplicated and search both systems by a stable identifier such as a tax ID, not by name. If the identifier matches and the names do not, you have found your seventh client.

Which two systems in your organisation disagree by one?
