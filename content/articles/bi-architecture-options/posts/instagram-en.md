Slow dashboards often start not in Power BI but in the architecture behind it.

Four BI architectures, compared:
1. Self-service on sources: live in days, breaks as data grows
2. Classic warehouse: one version of the truth, slow to change
3. Cloud warehouse + ELT: scales on demand, bill grows with volume
4. Lakehouse in Fabric: one copy of data, needs Fabric capacity

Power BI rules that pay off with any of them: star schema, one shared semantic model, Import by default.
