The Dupe Muncher ate four junk nodes in five seconds. Then it went for a real client.

Cleaning data is satisfying. NULL, TEST, N/A, a duplicate: gone, score goes up. The problem starts when the cleanup runs faster than the checking, and a real record looks junk enough to delete.

A quick check before any bulk delete: take ten records from the "delete" list and open each one by hand. If even one is real, the rule needs work, not the data.

Clean data is not less data. It is data you checked.

Ever deleted a real record by accident? Comment "oops".
