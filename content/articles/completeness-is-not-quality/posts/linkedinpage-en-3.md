How do you show data quality to leadership so the conversation turns to decisions rather than percentages?

My favourite technique is the simplest one: instead of one number, two columns, "filled in" and "usable". The gap between them is the real amount of work. Nobody needs a methodology lecture to see it.

The same gap shows where a report's conclusion may be distorted:
• coverage
• number of unique recipients
• amount paid

For the second column to exist at all, every critical field needs four things:
1. A business rule written in plain words.
2. A check that runs regularly, not once before the report.
3. The share of records that pass it, separate from completeness.
4. A person who fixes errors at the source, not in the report.

When the gap is large, the problem is rarely the analytics. More often there are no data entry rules and nobody responsible for the field.

A question for you: for your main register, do you know not only completeness but also the share of usable records?
