Two different revenue numbers in two reports usually point to the data architecture, not the people.

6 levels of data architecture, from simple to complex:
1. Reports straight from systems: live in days, different numbers
2. Replica (ODS): spares the ERP, logic still lives in reports
3. Warehouse and marts: one version of the truth, changes take weeks
4. Data lake: cheap and any format, a swamp without rules
5. Lakehouse: one copy for BI and ML, needs new skills
6. Data mesh: scales without a bottleneck, needs a mature organisation
