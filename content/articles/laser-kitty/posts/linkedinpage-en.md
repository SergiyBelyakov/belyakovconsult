A small arcade reminder for the start of the week.

Many data teams clean the same issues in the reporting layer every month: blank values, duplicates, placeholder dates, test records. The dashboard looks right, and next month the same issues arrive again from the same source.

A simple test: list the fixes your report applies every refresh. Each item on that list is a validation rule that belongs at the point of entry, in the form or source system, not in the report.

Fix the source, not the report.
