Why do capable teams keep a wrong number in a report for months?

Robert Cialdini's principles of influence explain it better than any data tool: authority ("the director quoted it"), social proof ("everyone uses this file"), consistency ("we reported it last month"), liking ("the dashboard looks great"), unity ("it is our team's number"), scarcity ("the only copy, do not touch") and reciprocity ("they helped me, I will not question their numbers").

None of these is evidence. A short habit helps: before a number goes into a decision, ask where it comes from and how it was checked, whoever brought it.

Which of the seven do you see most often in your organisation?
