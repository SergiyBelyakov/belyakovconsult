Data quality training does not have to be a slide deck.

Laser Kitty vs the Data Bugs is a free browser game built on the problems that show up in real registers: phone and date formats, impossible dates, placeholder values like 01.01.1900 or TEST TEST, duplicates written differently, and namesakes that must not be merged.

Each level ends with a quality score, a short review of mistakes and a tip for doing the same check in Excel or Power Query. Level 5 makes the main point: if the same errors keep arriving, fix the source, not the report.

Play: https://www.belyakovconsult.com/en/cases/laser-kitty/

Could this work as a warm-up for your data team?
