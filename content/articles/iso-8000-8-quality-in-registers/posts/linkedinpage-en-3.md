An address updated six months ago. Is that good quality data or not?

For an annual statistical report, yes. For a monthly payment by place of residence, no. Same data, opposite answers.

This is the third level of quality described in ISO 8000-8: pragmatic quality, meaning how fit the data is for the purpose of the people who use it. It is the only level that cannot be checked automatically against a specification. The system checks the format and the code list. Only the decision maker can judge fitness.

A detail that often gets missed: the standard sets no ready-made scales or thresholds. The organisation sets the threshold for each measured object according to its needs, and should state the scale it uses.

For a public register this means "good enough quality" does not appear on its own. Someone has to decide and write down:
• which decision the field is needed for
• who makes that decision
• what threshold is enough, for example how recent the data must be
• how often we measure

Naturally this is the register owner or its data steward, together with the people who work with the data.
