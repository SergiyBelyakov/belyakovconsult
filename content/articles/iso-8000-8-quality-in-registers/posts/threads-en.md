A value can pass every format check and still lead to a wrong payment. Because format is only the first of three quality levels described in ISO 8000-8.
<!-- next -->
Syntactic quality: does it match the format. Semantic: does it mean the right thing, by definition and code list. Pragmatic: is it fit for the decision. Registers usually measure only the first one.
