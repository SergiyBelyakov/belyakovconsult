Managers rarely open a dashboard. They do open a PDF in their inbox.

That is why the data quality dashboard demo builds a report in one click from the current filters. Three pages:
• key indicators and automatically calculated insights
• a region × domain quality matrix and the exceptions to review first
• data source health against the SLA
• the calculation methodology, so people who did not compute a figure can check it

A note on the quality target. On the same synthetic data, a 96% target flags 5 segments, 97% flags 94 and 98% flags 256. The threshold changes the picture by an order of magnitude, so the data owner sets it together with the decision makers and writes it into the methodology. The dashboard only shows the consequences.

The PDF is built in the browser; no data is sent anywhere. You can also upload your own CSV in the sample format.
