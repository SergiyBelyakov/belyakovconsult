GRWM: month-end report edition.

Most month-end reports don't fail at the chart. They fail earlier: test rows, duplicates, dates from 1900, three definitions of "active client", metrics nobody owns.

Each of these is a step from 0% to 100%. Skip them and the report still looks finished. It just changes after month-end.

A quick check before your next close: take one report and write down which of these six steps nobody owns. That's your 99%.
