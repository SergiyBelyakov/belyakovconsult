What we work on

What the work covers

We can start with one difficult decision or map the full data landscape. The point is to make the next move clear, not to produce a strategy document that sits on a shelf.

  • Review the current data, reporting and system landscape.
  • Define a target data and analytics model linked to management decisions.
  • Prioritise initiatives by value, risk, dependency and delivery effort.
  • Build a roadmap with clear stages, owners and readiness criteria.
  • Provide an independent review of an existing strategy or technical concept.
