Demo · anomaly detection in 3D

Anomalies a dashboard cannot show

Almost 6,000 payments made by a fictional organisation in a year. The monthly report is green and the deviation from plan is under one percent. Now the same data as a cloud of records: each point is a payment, unusual ones glow ruby. Rotate the cloud, zoom in and fly through it. Click a point to see why the record was flagged, how strong the deviation is and why it may be normal rather than an error or fraud. The data is synthetic; the method is real.

What is inside

Five pages: the record cloud, which unfolds from a regular dashboard; a list of signals with reasons; threshold and review (how many errors and risks are found, how many rare but normal cases, a comparison of three methods: feature deviation, isolation forest, nearest neighbours; a group’s own baseline or a shared one); anomaly search in your own CSV or Excel file right in the browser with a download of suspicious records; the method.

Learning · a data quality game

Laser Kitty vs the Data Bugs

An arcade game in the spirit of old cabinets: record bugs fly in formation and dive at the warehouse, and a kitten with laser eyes deals with them. The errors are real: 31 February, month 13, test@test.com, 01.01.1900, a phone in five formats, the same person with an initial instead of a first name. The laser deletes impossible values and placeholders, the fix beam normalises formats, the merge beam fuses duplicates, and correct records must be let through.

What is inside

Five levels: formats, impossible values, duplicates and namesakes, placeholders, and a source that keeps sending errors until you seal it with an entry rule. After each level: warehouse quality, skills and an explanation of every mistake with a tip for doing the same in Excel or Power Query; level records download as CSV. A calm mode without return fire, keyboard and touch controls.
