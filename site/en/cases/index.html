Demo · analytics work sample

A data quality dashboard you can try

This is the kind of working tool I build for data teams: processing, quality and source availability indicators, a region × domain matrix and the exceptions worth reviewing first. The data is synthetic; the indicator logic is real.

What is inside

Six pages: overview, analytics, data quality, sources, reports and appearance. The quality target moves with a slider, filters drive every chart, and a management PDF report is built from the current filters in one click. You can upload your own CSV: everything is calculated in the browser and the file goes nowhere.

Demo · graph analytics

A network of ties and a need index

The graph shows what separate tables hide: who lives without a support network, who holds care together in a community, where social workers are overloaded and which records may be duplicates. The data is synthetic; the indicator logic is real.

What is inside

Six pages: a network of people, households, social workers and clinics; a map of co-occurring demographic, social and medical characteristics; a composite index with weights you can change; a comparison of communities; signals for social work; the method and the rules for protecting sensitive data. Nodes and ties can be exported to CSV for Gephi or Neo4j.

04

Financial Analytics

Financial control becomes difficult when budgets, payments, donor funding, accounts receivable and reporting sit in separate files. The problem is usually not a lack of numbers. It is the lack of one traceable financial picture.

What I do

I bring financial flows, plan versus actual, donor funding sources, receivables and obligations into a single model. It can then support recurring reporting, variance analysis and practical control over the use of funds.

05

Management Analytics

Internal business processes are often spread across many spreadsheets, emails and local reports. Management can see individual figures, but not always where a process is slowing down, what is overdue or where intervention is needed.

What I do

I build a common analytical model for plans and tasks, procurement, deadlines, team workload, operating cycles, KPIs and deviations. Data from different functions is brought into one management view that can be used regularly, not only at reporting time.
