What we work on

What the work covers

The work starts with the indicator, not the chart. Source, formula, period, grain, owner and quality checks come first. That is what makes BI stable.

  • Define indicators, formulas, sources and aggregation rules.
  • Normalise Excel and other inputs into analysis ready tables.
  • Build the data model, Power Query, DAX and semantic layer.
  • Design executive and operational dashboards plus print ready reports where needed.
  • Add data quality checks before refresh and make numbers reproducible.
