Stages of the journey

13 stages from source to decision

What the scene above shows, in text: what happens at each stage and what it delivers.

  1. 01
    DATA STRATEGY

    Start with the decision.

    Define who needs the outcome and which decision it should support. Assess the current state and agree priorities.

    Result. Agreed questions, accountable owners and an implementation roadmap.

    About the service: Data Strategy
  2. 02
    DIFFERENT SOURCES

    Many systems. Different contexts.

    Transactions, customers, files, documents, registries and geospatial sources each hold part of the picture. Establish availability, structure, ownership and refresh frequency.

    Result. A catalog of sources, critical fields and conditions for obtaining data.

    About the service: Data ArchitectureSee it in the Data Lab →
  3. 03
    INTEGRATION AND EXCHANGE

    The flow must be repeatable.

    Connect sources, agree API contracts and implement batch loads or CDC. Each task has a status, a log and recovery rules.

    Result. Managed ingestion with a known source for every record.

    About the service: Data ArchitectureSee it in the Data Lab →
  4. 04
    QUALITY AND CORRECTION

    Every error has a route.

    Validate formats, completeness, duplicates and business rules. Reconcile reference data. Assign failed records for correction, then validate them again.

    Result. Validated datasets and a visible queue of quality incidents.

    About the service: Data QualitySee it in the Data Lab →
  5. 05
    STORAGE AND MODELING

    A consistent structure emerges.

    Separate raw and prepared data, design facts and dimensions, retain the required history and build business data marts. Select an architecture that fits the work.

    Result. A clear data structure with the necessary history and dependencies.

    About the service: Data Architecture
  6. 06
    RULES ACROSS THE JOURNEY

    Trust requires accountability.

    Agree definitions, owners, roles and usage policies. Document datasets and measure lineage. Control access, changes and delivery.

    Result. Agreed rules, traceability and role-based access.

    About the service: Data GovernanceSee it in the Data Lab →
  7. 07
    DATA ANALYSIS

    First understand what the numbers mean.

    Explore distributions, relationships and deviations. Compare segments and periods. Validate forecasting and scenario models when the data supports them.

    Result. Findings with an explanation of the method and its limitations.

    About the service: Data AnalysisSee it in the Data Lab →
  8. 08
    BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

    Every measure has a method and a source.

    Build a semantic model, agree KPIs and prepare dashboards and reports. Users can drill through to the records behind a measure.

    Result. Reusable measures and understandable analytical products.

    About the service: Business IntelligenceSee it in the Data Lab →
  9. 09
    FINANCIAL ANALYTICS

    What drives the financial result?

    Connect accounting with budgets and analytical dimensions. Investigate revenue, spending, margin, cash flows and the consequences of scenario assumptions.

    Result. A financial model, plan-vs-actual analysis and explained variances.

    About the service: Financial AnalyticsSee it in the Data Lab →
  10. 10
    MANAGEMENT ANALYTICS

    Where does the process lose its outcome?

    Compare goals, workloads, cycle times and resources. Show deviations within each team's responsibility and identify the action required.

    Result. Process measures, priorities and accountable actions.

    About the service: Management AnalyticsSee it in the Data Lab →
  11. 11
    GEOSPATIAL ANALYSIS

    Where is the need? Where is the capacity?

    Link measures to territories and service networks. Validate addresses and analyze coverage, resource locations and accessibility using agreed methods.

    Result. Spatial findings for networks, logistics and regional planning.

    About the service: Geospatial AnalysisSee it in the Data Lab →
  12. 12
    USERS AND CHANNELS

    One foundation. Different ways to work.

    Leadership receives a summary. Specialists work with detail. Analysts investigate causes. Auditors verify lineage, while applications use agreed APIs.

    Result. The right outcome for each role through an authorized channel.

    About the service: Business Intelligence
  13. 13
    OPERATIONS AND OUTCOMES

    Every action creates new data.

    Monitor ingestion, freshness and quality. Handle incidents, train users and review the results of actions. New questions inform the next priorities.

    Result. A working data system with controls and a cycle of improvement.

    About the service: Management Analytics
