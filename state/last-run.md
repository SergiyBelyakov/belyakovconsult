"How many service recipients do you have?"

Three teams can easily give three different answers, and all of them will be correct. One counts people, another counts applications, a third counts people who received the service at least once a year.

It is a recipe without grams: "some flour, sugar to taste". Everyone gets a cake, but a different one. (The analogy has limits: nobody asks a cake why it got smaller than in the last report.)

The tool against this is simple and boring, which is why it works: an indicator reference sheet. Five lines:
1. What exactly we count, in one sentence.
2. Unit: person, application or service.
3. Period and the date we count on.
4. Data source and who is responsible for it.
5. A formula someone else can repeat.

A 10-minute task: take one indicator from your last report and fill in those five lines. If you had to call someone at line three, you need the sheet more than you thought.
